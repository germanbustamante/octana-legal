Octana privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Octana is an app to compare fuel prices at nearby stations. It has no accounts and no ads, and it never sells or shares your data for advertising. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Germán de Bustamante Conde (“we”), the developer of Octana, is the data controller. Contact: octana.support@gmail.com.

What the app handles

DataWhyLegal basis

Your preferences, favourite stations and recently loaded prices are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Who else processes it

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.): usage statistics, only with your consent, and crash reports (Crashlytics).
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud): usage statistics, only with your consent. It also tells the app whether it is under maintenance, which uses a random identifier and no personal data.
  • Render and Neon: hosting of our server and its database, in the EU.
  • Google Maps (Android) and Apple Maps (iOS) draw the map; they receive the area shown, as any map does.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. They do so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

Usage statistics: at most 14 months. Crash reports: 90 days. Server logs: a few days. Data on your device: until you delete the app or its data.

Your choices and rights

  • You decide on usage statistics when you first open the app, and can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics.
  • Location is optional: the app works by choosing a province instead.
  • You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its use, or ask for a copy, by writing to octana.support@gmail.com. As we don't know who you are, include the installation id if you have it, or tell us what you need and we will help.
  • You can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, or the one where you live.

Children

Octana is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
