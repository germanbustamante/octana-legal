# octana-legal

The public pages of the Octana app: its privacy policy (English and Spanish) and a support page,
served by GitHub Pages at https://germanbustamante.github.io/octana-legal/. The app links here from
Settings and its onboarding (`LegalLinks` in octana-app), and the store listings use these URLs.

Plain HTML, no build step. The policy follows octana-app's `docs/PRIVACY.md`, the engineering
inventory of what the app collects: change both together, and update the date at the top.
